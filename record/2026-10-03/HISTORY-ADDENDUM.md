# My history with the method: the earlier dates

Joshua Bauer (ISWT42). Sealed 3 October 2026.

The record I sealed earlier tonight starts the history in August. My own dated records go back further. My report The Watched Check, version 2.1 of 29 September 2026, lists them, and it is public at https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0430c813-d194-420d-93fc-67a6f4555029.

- **24 June 2026.** My go/no-go gate for my projects, at that time also called the Sonny Test (an older, separate use of the name), states: "a gate you cannot fail is not a gate". It is the root of my rule that a checker that never says "fail" proves nothing.
- **28 July 2026.** A note on a coding agent running with full access, no approval prompts and no sandbox. It concludes that file permissions alone cannot fix that.
- **30 July 2026.** A note on an earlier incident with concurrent agent sessions on one machine. The lesson recorded: an approval rule inside one session cannot bind a different session.
- **13 August 2026.** A rule in force: "a check that cannot fail is not evidence".
- **29 August 2026.** My "Win conditions" charter. It keeps its flagship idea unnamed in every working session, on purpose: the same method I used to keep the test's purpose from the models.
- **30–31 August 2026.** Negative controls required when validating a gate, and the principle "evidence requires a failure control".
- **4 September 2026.** Planted negative controls exposed a test runner that always passed.
- **8–9 September 2026.** My brief for a file-gate test, with acceptance condition AC4: "A prompt asking the agent to avoid other tools is not sufficient evidence", and if the condition can't be met, "report that condition as blocked".
- **9 September 2026.** The six-session run that the report is about.

ISWT, In Sonny We Trust, is a motto I used before this work. The 29 September report also records my wish that the method carry Sonny's name, the way Schrödinger's cat names a thought experiment. The method is called The Watched Check, and at its heart is the Sonny Test.

These dates come from my files, as the report documents them. This addendum seals them on this date.
